## 5. Prohibited Promotional Practices

Partners may not:
- Claim or imply any Manus (-8) product includes live sessions, coaching, a cohort, group access, or community access. This applies with particular force to The AI Business Mastermind -8, per `SELF-GUIDED-COPY-PATCHES.md`.
- Make income, earnings, or business-outcome guarantees on behalf of any product.
- Imply Mara Quinn is a real human being, or omit her AI-educator disclosure in any promotional content.
- Bid on the brand's own name or close misspellings in paid search (trademark/brand-bidding restriction) — **OWNER DECISION REQUIRED** on whether this is enforced, since brand terms and legal entity name are not yet finalized.
- Use spam, unsolicited bulk email, or misleading subject lines to drive clicks.
- Represent themselves as an employee, founder, or official spokesperson of Manus (-8) unless specifically authorized.
