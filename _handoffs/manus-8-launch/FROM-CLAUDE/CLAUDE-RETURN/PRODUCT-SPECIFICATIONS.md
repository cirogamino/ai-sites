## 1. The Daily AI Prompt Club -8 — $9

- **Buyer:** A beginner-to-intermediate AI user who wants a daily practice habit, not a course to finish once.
- **Problem:** Owns AI tools but uses them inconsistently and doesn't know what to try next.
- **Transformation:** 30 days of guided, distinct daily practice that builds a personal, tested prompt library from real use — not a copied list.
- **Exact inclusions:** `START-HERE.md`, `30-DAY-PROMPT-PRACTICE.md` (30 distinct daily entries: purpose, ready-to-use prompt, short explanation, practice challenge, completion check), `PROMPT-PRACTICE-TRACKER.csv` (30 rows + header), `QUICK-REFERENCE.md` (the four-part prompt formula from the free lead magnet, one page).
- **Exclusions:** No live sessions, no community, no 1:1 feedback, no software or automation. No guarantee of income or productivity gain.
- **Delivery format:** Digital download (ZIP), Markdown + CSV. Readable in any text editor or spreadsheet app.
- **Access duration:** Lifetime access to the delivered files. No subscription, no expiring link (subject to Manus's actual delivery mechanism — see Fulfillment Manifest).
- **Support boundary:** Async email support for delivery/access issues only. Not a coaching or consulting service.
- **Disclaimers:** Mara Quinn is a disclosed virtual persona. No outcome, income, or time-savings is promised. Results depend entirely on the buyer's own use.
- **Refund assumption:** Digital product, immediately accessible on delivery — see `REFUND-AND-FULFILLMENT-POLICY-DRAFT.md` for the standard applied (owner/legal review required before publishing).
- **Version:** v1.0, 2026-08-04.
- **Acceptance criteria:** 30 rows in the tracker CSV; 30 genuinely distinct prompts (no repeated task type on consecutive days); every day has all five required fields; file opens cleanly in Excel/Sheets/Numbers.

## 2. The AI Automation Templates -8 — $49

- **Buyer:** A small business owner or operator who has one repeated workflow worth simplifying and wants a structured, low-risk way to test AI assistance on it.
- **Problem:** Doesn't know how to scope an automation pilot without either overbuilding or automating a process nobody's mapped.
- **Transformation:** A complete document set to take one real workflow from "we do this every week" to a reviewed, evidence-backed pilot decision.
- **Exact inclusions:** `START-HERE.md`, `AUTOMATION-TEMPLATE-PACK.md` (14 components: opportunity card, workflow map, task brief, source packet, output contract, review checklist, exception log, decision receipt, test charter, scale gate, rollback plan, operating cadence, training record, contribution ledger), `AUTOMATION-PILOT-TRACKER.csv` (supports one complete pilot end-to-end), `QUICK-REFERENCE.md` (one-page version of the Automation Readiness Map scoring system).
- **Exclusions:** No software, no built automations, no vendor recommendations, no implementation service. This is a planning and testing framework, not the automation itself.
- **Delivery format:** Digital download (ZIP), Markdown + CSV.
- **Access duration:** Lifetime access to delivered files.
- **Support boundary:** Async email support for delivery/access issues only.
- **Disclaimers:** No business outcome, cost reduction, or time savings is guaranteed. Templates require the buyer's own workflow knowledge to complete.
- **Refund assumption:** Same standard as above — see policy draft.
- **Version:** v1.0, 2026-08-04.
- **Acceptance criteria:** All 14 named components present in the template pack; tracker CSV supports intake through decision for at least one pilot; no step assumes software the buyer hasn't chosen yet.

## 3. The AI Business Mastermind -8 — $199

- **Buyer:** A business owner or operator who needs a structured decision process for adopting AI in their operation — not a course, not a community.
- **Problem:** Faces AI adoption decisions without a repeatable framework for scoping, testing, and evaluating outcomes with evidence rather than gut feel.
- **Transformation:** A self-guided 30-day program that walks one real workflow through weekly decisions, evidence reviews, correction-load tracking, and a final continue/revise/narrow/pause/stop call.
- **Exact inclusions:** `START-HERE.md`, `30-DAY-DECISION-ROOM.md` (4 weekly decision cycles + final review), `OPERATOR-SCORECARD.csv` (weekly evidence and correction-load tracking), `QUICK-REFERENCE.md` (the five-decision canvas from the free lead magnet, one page).
- **Exclusions — explicit and non-negotiable per build instructions:** **No live sessions, no cohort, no group calls, no community access, no 1:1 coaching, no guaranteed outcome.** This product is immediately deliverable on purchase with no scheduled event of any kind.
- **⚠️ Known inconsistency, flagged for owner decision (see QA-REPORT.md):** the existing lead magnet 3 and the live storefront both describe this product as a "working room... with other operators" — language that reads as live community/cohort access. This specification and the built product deliberately do NOT include that, because the build instructions explicitly forbid promising community/coaching/live access unless the owner authorizes it, and no such authorization is in the source files. **The marketing copy needs to be reconciled with the actual product before launch — either soften "room"/"with other operators" to reflect the self-guided reality, or decide to actually build that feature.** This is an owner decision, not something Claude or Manus should silently resolve either direction.
- **Delivery format:** Digital download (ZIP), Markdown + CSV.
- **Access duration:** Lifetime access to delivered files.
- **Support boundary:** Async email support for delivery/access issues only.
- **Disclaimers:** No business, revenue, or efficiency outcome is guaranteed — carried directly from the lead magnet's own disclaimer. Mara Quinn is a disclosed virtual persona throughout.
- **Refund assumption:** Same standard — see policy draft.
- **Version:** v1.0, 2026-08-04.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Program runs fully self-guided with no scheduled event; four weekly cycles plus final decision are all present; scorecard tracks evidence and correction load, not just task completion; no language implies live access, coaching, or community.
