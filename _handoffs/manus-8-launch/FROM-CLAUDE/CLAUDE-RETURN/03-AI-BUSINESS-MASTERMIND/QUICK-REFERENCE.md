# Quick Reference — The AI Business Mastermind -8

## The Weekly Cycle
1. **Frame it** — one sentence, real deadline.
2. **Pressure-test it** — every real option, top risk each, name the actual constraint.
3. **Make the call** — decision + reversal trigger + who's informed.
4. **Act and watch** — first real action, check the trigger, note what you'd change.
5. *(Month-end only)* **Review the pattern**, not just the decisions.

## The Reversal Trigger Formula
> "I will reverse this decision if [specific, observable thing] happens by [date]."

If you can't fill this in, the decision isn't ready to make yet.

## The Four Real Constraints
Money, time, energy/nerve, and information/skill. Most people default to blaming money — check the other three before you accept that answer.

## The Operator's Rule
> A decision without a written reversal trigger isn't a decision — it's a hope.
