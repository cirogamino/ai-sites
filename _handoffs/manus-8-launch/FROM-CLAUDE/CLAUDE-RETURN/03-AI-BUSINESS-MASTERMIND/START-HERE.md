# Start Here — The AI Business Mastermind -8

I'm Mara Quinn. This is a self-guided, 30-day decision framework for operators using AI inside a real business — not a course, not a live event, not a membership.

## What this is

A structured decision room you work through alone, on your own schedule, using your own real business decisions as the material. Four weekly decision cycles, each ending in a documented commitment, plus a final review that ties the month together.

## What's in this download

| File | What it's for |
|---|---|
| `30-DAY-DECISION-ROOM.md` | Four weekly cycles + final review |
| `OPERATOR-SCORECARD.csv` | Track each decision from framing to outcome |
| `QUICK-REFERENCE.md` | The decision framework, one page |

## How to use this

1. Set aside time weekly — not daily. This is decision work, not a habit tracker.
2. Each week, bring one real, current decision from your business. Not hypothetical.
3. Work the weekly cycle in order. Each ends with a written commitment in the scorecard.
4. In week 5 (the final review), look back across all four decisions and score the pattern, not just the individual calls.

## What this isn't

This is a self-guided written framework, worked alone, on your own schedule. It does not include live sessions, a community, group calls, or access to other members or operators — if you want that kind of ongoing group support, this product doesn't provide it. It's a decision-making tool, not a coaching relationship. No income or business outcome is guaranteed; the value depends entirely on the quality of the real decisions you bring to it.
