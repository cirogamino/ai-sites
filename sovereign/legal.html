Legal, privacy and terms

Plain-language terms for working with Sovereign. Written to be read, not to hide behind.

Last updated 5 August 2026

Who we are

Sovereign is an AI phone and follow-up service for concrete and flatwork contractors, operated by Ciro Gamino in Wisconsin, United States.

Contact: sovereign@cirogamino.com

The numbers on this site

Every dollar figure on this site — the calculator, the sample quote, the recovery ranges — is a planning estimate built from industry averages. They are not promises, projections, or guarantees of revenue.

Your actual results depend on your call volume, your close rate, your pricing, and your market. Nothing here is financial advice.

How the AI identifies itself

When Sovereign answers your phone, it identifies itself as the scheduling line for your company. It does not claim to be a specific human employee.

Sovereign qualifies the job, books a site walk, and sends a confirmation. It never quotes pour pricing and never commits you to a price. You stay the closer.

Calls may be recorded and transcribed so you can review them in your portal. You are responsible for any call-recording notice your state requires; we provide the notice language and enable it on request.

Communications

We do not send cold text messages to cell phones. Text messages go only to people who called your business or asked to be contacted.

  • Marketing email follows CAN-SPAM: real sender, real address, working unsubscribe.
  • Outbound AI voice contact is limited to people who already engaged, during business hours.
  • Every message includes a way to stop receiving them, and stop requests are honored immediately.

Your data

What we collect. When you submit the access form: your name, company, phone, email, city, the package you selected, and any notes you write. When Sovereign runs on your line: caller phone numbers, call recordings and transcripts, and booking details.

Why we collect it. To respond to your request, to operate the service you bought, and to show you your own call and booking history.

Who we share it with. The infrastructure providers required to run the service — telephony, AI model providers, calendar, email delivery, and hosting. We do not sell your data or your customers' data to anyone, for any purpose.

How long we keep it. Access requests are kept for up to 24 months. Call recordings and transcripts are kept for 12 months unless you ask for a shorter window. Everything is deleted within 30 days of a written deletion request.

Your rights. Email us to see, correct, export, or delete anything we hold about you. No account required and no fee.

Cookies. This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. Your light or dark theme preference and the sample quote timer are stored locally in your own browser and never sent to us.

Service terms

  • Billing. Monthly plans bill monthly in advance. One-time build fees are due before work begins.
  • Cancellation. Monthly plans can be cancelled any time after the initial setup term. Cancel before your renewal date and you are not charged again.
  • Refunds. Monthly fees already billed are not refunded, but you keep service through the period you paid for. Build fees become non-refundable once configuration work has started.
  • Go-live. The 72-hour target starts when payment clears and we have access to your phone number and calendar. Delays in that access move the date.
  • What you own. Your customer data, call records, and phone number are yours. Export them any time. The Sovereign scripts, prompts, and platform stay ours.
  • Uptime. Sovereign depends on third-party telephony and AI providers. We monitor and respond quickly, but we do not offer an uptime guarantee at these price points.
  • Limit of liability. Our total liability is capped at the amount you paid us in the previous three months.

Not legal advice

Guidance we share about call recording, consent, or contact rules is operational, based on how we run the service. It is not attorney advice. Check your own obligations in your state.

Changes

If these terms change in a way that affects active customers, we email you before the change takes effect. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
